VERDARA — Responsive Botanical Skincare Website

Files:
- index.html: page content and sections
- style.css: responsive styling, layout, and animations
- script.js: mobile navigation, demo add-to-bag, testimonial slider, newsletter feedback, reveal effects

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts, Lucide icons, and the Unsplash product photography.

Customize before publishing:
- Change VERDARA branding and logo in index.html.
- Replace the Unsplash image URLs with your own product photography.
- Update product names, pricing, claims, and reviews to match your real business.
- The bag and newsletter are front-end demos only; connect them to a store backend or email service for real orders/subscriptions.
